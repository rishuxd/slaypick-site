Toybox Blast

Aim. Fire. Smash the candy tower.

How to play

Tap where you want to hit and the cannon fires a candy ball exactly there. Knock every jar, glass block and toffee brick off the table before your shots run out. Spare shots earn more stars.

Special balls

  • Triple splits into three balls in mid-air.
  • Jelly bounces all over the table.
  • Bomb blasts everything near where it lands.
  • Jawbreaker is huge and heavy enough to shove blocks right off.
