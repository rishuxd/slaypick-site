Privacy Policy

Toybox Blast · Effective 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Toybox Blast game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. Toybox Blast is made by Slay Pick. If you have any question, contact us at jyotiswaroopwork@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, balls, cannons, settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice any time in Settings.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your phone using the standard storage provided by the game engine (Unity). This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to get an extra shot, double coins or a free ball) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game automatically collects:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

This data is collected and processed by Google, is encrypted in transit, and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads shown in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to play levels. To check that you are online, it regularly sends a small request to Google's connectivity check service. This request contains no personal information from the game, and we don't store anything from it.

Age

Toybox Blast is intended for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share any information with third parties ourselves. The only third party involved is Google, as the ad provider described above.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time, and you can contact us with any request about your data and we will help you exercise your rights.

Security

The game does not run its own servers and does not collect personal information itself. Ad-related data is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about privacy in Toybox Blast, email jyotiswaroopwork@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
