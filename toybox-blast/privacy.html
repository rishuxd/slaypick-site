Privacy Policy

Toybox Blast · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Toybox Blast game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. Toybox Blast is made by Slay Pick. If you have any question, contact us at slaypick99@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your email, phone number or a password. You can play without signing in.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, balls, cannons, settings) is saved on your device.
  • Endless mode has an online leaderboard. It stores a player name, your best endless scores and an anonymous player ID on Google Firebase. If you choose to connect Google Play Games, your Play Games name is used as your leaderboard name.
  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and Firebase Crashlytics to fix crashes. Neither includes your name or contact details.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice any time in Settings.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your phone using the standard storage provided by the game engine (Unity). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Online leaderboard (Google Firebase)

When you play endless mode, the game signs you in to Google Firebase Authentication anonymously: Firebase creates a random player ID for your installation, with no name, email or other details. Your leaderboard entry, stored in Google Cloud Firestore, contains:

  • Your random player ID
  • Your leaderboard name: an automatic name such as "Guest 1234", or your Google Play Games display name if you connect Google Play Games
  • Your best wave and score, overall and for the current week, and when it was last updated

Your leaderboard name, wave and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Nothing else in your entry is shown to anyone.

Google Play Games (optional)

Connecting Google Play Games is optional; you can do it from Settings or the leaderboard screen. If you connect, Google Play Games shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the game so that your leaderboard entry is linked to your Play Games profile and keeps the same name on your other devices. We do not receive your email address or your contacts. Google Play Games is provided by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the game we use Firebase Analytics, which records gameplay events such as levels started and finished, stars earned, shots used, ads watched, coins spent and the furthest level reached, along with standard device information (device model, OS version, country, app version) and an app-instance identifier. We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which include the device model, OS version, the state of the game when it crashed and a crash identifier. We use this data only in aggregate, to fix bugs and make the game better. It does not include your name or contact details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to get an extra shot, double coins, a free ball or to keep going in endless mode) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game automatically collects:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

This data is collected and processed by Google, is encrypted in transit, and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads shown in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in: connecting Google Play Games is optional. Without it, your leaderboard name is an automatic "Guest" name.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to play levels. To check that you are online, it regularly sends a small request to Google's connectivity check service. This request contains no personal information from the game, and we don't store anything from it.

Age

Toybox Blast is intended for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will remove it.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. The service providers that process data for the game are Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play Games and AdMob), each as described above. Your leaderboard name and scores are visible to other players.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Your leaderboard entry is kept while the leaderboard is running. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, under Firebase's standard retention. To delete your leaderboard entry and the data linked to your player ID, email slaypick99@gmail.com with the subject "Toybox Blast data deletion" and your leaderboard name; we will delete it within 30 days. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies, and you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

Security

All data the game sends (leaderboard, analytics, crash reports and ads) travels over encrypted connections to Google's services. Leaderboard entries can only be written by the player they belong to.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about privacy in Toybox Blast, email slaypick99@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
