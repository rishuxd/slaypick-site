Privacy Policy

Feast Street · Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Feast Street restaurant game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. Feast Street is made by Slay Pick. If you have any question, contact us at slaypick99@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your game progress (coins, restaurant level, unlocked areas, upgrades, staff and skills) is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your phone using the standard storage provided by the game engine (Unity). This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location or usage data. It works fully offline.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any information, because the game does not collect any.

Contacting us by email

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send, including your email address. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that use your information, such as ads or analytics, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about privacy in Feast Street, email slaypick99@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
