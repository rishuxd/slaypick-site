Wordlings

Find the hidden words. Travel the worlds.

How to play

Drag across the letters, or tap the first letter and then the last, to circle a hidden word. Words hide across, down, diagonally and, in harder levels, backwards. Find every word to finish the level. Using fewer hints earns more stars.

What's inside

  • Adventure: three themed worlds, Jungle Trail, Ocean Deep and Space, with grids that grow from easy to hard.
  • Daily Puzzle: a fresh puzzle every day. Keep your streak going.
  • Word Book: collect a word card with a short meaning for every level you finish.
  • Nearby party: pass one phone around and race 2 to 4 friends on the same grid.
  • Plays offline: adventure, daily puzzles and nearby party all work without internet.
