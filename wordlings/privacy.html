Privacy Policy

Wordlings · Effective 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Wordlings word search game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. Wordlings is made by Slay Pick. If you have any question, contact us at slaypick99@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. We don't ask for your email, phone number or any contact details.
  • Your profile name, game progress (levels, stars, coins, streak, word cards) and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which parts of the game work, which levels are too hard, and why the game crashes.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice any time in Settings.

Information stored on your device

The name you type for your profile, your colour, your progress and your settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The "Read words aloud" feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The game only passes it the puzzle words.

Nearby party is played by passing one device between players. It doesn't connect to other devices or to the internet.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for a hint, to skip a level, or to save your streak) and full-screen ads after you finish a level. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game automatically collects:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

Ads shown in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teenagers.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the game we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. When your device is online they collect:

  • Gameplay events: screens you open, levels started, finished or left (with time taken, words found and stars), hints, level skips, streak saves, whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end, and taps on "Rate us" and "Send feedback"
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and an app instance ID created by Firebase
  • Advertising ID, where your device makes it available
  • Approximate location (country or region), estimated from your IP address
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, the last screen you were on, device state such as free memory, and a random installation identifier

We see this information only as statistics and crash reports. It does not include your profile name and is not used to identify you.

Advertising, analytics and crash data are collected and processed by Google, encrypted in transit, and used for the purposes above. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback, your email app opens a message to us with your app version and device model already filled in. We receive only what you choose to send, including your email address. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Play offline: the game works without internet. While offline, no ads, analytics or crash reports are sent.

Age

Wordlings is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google, as the provider of AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics described above.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for no more than 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days. Advertising data is handled under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time, and you can contact us with any request about your data and we will help you exercise your rights.

Security

The game does not run its own servers for single-player play and does not collect your name or contact details. Ad, analytics and crash data is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when online multiplayer launches, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about privacy in Wordlings, email slaypick99@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
